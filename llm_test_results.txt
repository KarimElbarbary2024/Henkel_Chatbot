Q: Tell me about Safari
A: Safari is the web browser you can use on your iPhone to browse the web. It allows you to collect webpages to read later using the Reading List feature, and you can add page icons to your Home screen for quick access. With iCloud, you can see pages you have open on other devices and keep your bookmarks and reading list updated across them. You can enter a web address or search item by tapping, zoom in or out by double-tapping or pinching, and navigate through your bookmarks, reading list, browsing history, and tweeted links. You can also swipe through open pages or open a new page. Safari offers various ways to share or save content as well [Source: Page 55 | Safari at a glance]. 

Additionally, you can customize Safari settings by going to Settings > Safari, where you can choose your search engine, provide AutoFill information, manage favorites, block pop-ups, tighten privacy and security, and clear your history, cookies, and data. You can also enable the Fraudulent Website Warning to watch for suspicious websites [Source: Page 59 | Safari settings].

Q: My iPhone is frozen, what do I do?
A: If your iPhone is frozen, you can try to force restart it. To do this, hold down the Sleep/Wake button and the Home button at the same time for at least ten seconds, until the Apple logo appears. If that doesn't work, you may need to reset your iPhone or try other troubleshooting steps like restarting the device or forcing an app to quit. [Source: Page 154 | Restart or reset iPhone]

Q: How do I send a text message?
A: To send a text message on your iPhone, follow these steps:

1. Tap the Messages app.
2. Tap the compose button (usually represented by a pencil icon).
3. Enter a phone number or email address, or tap to choose a contact.
4. Type your message in the text field.
5. Tap the send button to send your message.

You can also include a photo or video by tapping the attach media button before sending your message. [Source: Page 67 | Send and receive messages]

Q: How do I connect to Wi-Fi?
A: To connect to Wi-Fi on your iPhone, follow these steps:

1. Go to Settings > Wi-Fi.
2. Choose a network by tapping one of the listed networks and enter the password if asked.
3. If you want to be prompted when a Wi-Fi network is available, turn on “Ask to Join Networks.” Otherwise, you must manually join a network when a previously used network isn’t available.
4. If you need to join a closed Wi-Fi network, tap Other, then enter the name of the closed network, along with the security type and password if required.
5. To forget a network you’ve joined before, tap next to the network and then tap “Forget this Network.”
6. You can also adjust the settings for a Wi-Fi network by tapping next to the network to set an HTTP proxy, define static network settings, turn on BootP, or renew the settings provided by a DHCP server.

Once connected, a Wi-Fi icon will appear at the top of the screen, indicating that you are connected to a Wi-Fi network. Your iPhone will reconnect automatically whenever you return to the same location. [Source: Page 15 | Connect to Wi-Fi]

Q: How do I back up my iPhone?
A: You can back up your iPhone using iCloud or iTunes. If you choose to back up using iCloud, you cannot also use iTunes for automatic backups, but you can use iTunes for manual backups. iCloud backs up your iPhone daily over Wi-Fi when it is connected to a power source and is locked. 

To turn on iCloud backups, go to Settings > iCloud, log in with your Apple ID and password if required, then go to Storage & Backup and turn on iCloud Backup. 

To turn on backups in iTunes, go to File > Devices > Back Up. 

iCloud backs up various data, including purchased music, movies, TV shows, apps, books, photos and videos in your Camera Roll, iPhone settings, app data, home screen layout, messages, and ringtones [Source: Page 156 | Back up iPhone].

Q: How do I use Siri?

You can ask Siri anything, such as setting a timer or checking movie showtimes. Siri understands natural speech, so you don’t need to learn special commands. You can also manually control when Siri listens by holding down the Home button while you speak and releasing it when you're done. For hints on what Siri can do, you can ask, "what can you do," or tap the screen for more options [Source: Page 41 | Make requests].

Q: How do I set a passcode?
A: To set a passcode on your iPhone, go to Settings > Touch ID & Passcode (for iPhone 5s) or Settings > Passcode (for other models), and set a 4-digit passcode. Setting a passcode will turn on data protection, using your passcode as a key to encrypt Mail messages and attachments stored on your iPhone. [Source: Page 37 | Security]

Q: How do I turn on Bluetooth?
A: To turn on Bluetooth on your iPhone, go to Settings > Bluetooth. [Source: Page 35 | Bluetooth devices]
