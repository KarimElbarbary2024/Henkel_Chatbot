Q: How do I turn on Do Not Disturb?
A: To turn on Do Not Disturb, swipe up from the bottom edge of the screen to open Control Center, then tap the Do Not Disturb icon. When Do Not Disturb is on, the icon appears in the status bar. [Source: Page 32 | Do Not Disturb]

Q: How do I set up iCloud?
A: To set up iCloud, you can do it when you first set up your iPhone or later in Settings. To set up an iCloud account later, go to Settings > iCloud. [Source: Page 16 | Set up mail and other accounts]

Q: Tell me about Safari
A: Safari is the web browser on iPhone that allows you to browse the web. You can use the Reading List feature to collect webpages to read later and add page icons to your Home screen for quick access. With iCloud, you can see pages you have open on other devices and keep your bookmarks and reading list updated across all your devices. You can enter a web address or search item by tapping, zoom in by double-tapping or pinching, and swipe through open pages or open a new page. Additionally, you can choose from various ways to share or save content and revisit recent pages [Source: Page 55 | Safari at a glance]. 

In the Safari settings, you can manage features such as choosing your search engine, providing AutoFill information, selecting which favorites to display, having links open in a new page or in the background, blocking pop-ups, tightening privacy and security, and clearing your history, cookies, and data [Source: Page 59 | Safari settings].

Q: How do I use AirDrop?
A: To use AirDrop, you can share photos, videos, and files wirelessly with nearby devices. AirDrop requires an iCloud account and works within approximately 30 feet (10 meters). It uses Wi-Fi and Bluetooth and is available in Control Center. In many apps, you can tap Share or to see sharing and other options, which may vary depending on the app you’re using. To share with a nearby friend using AirDrop, simply follow the prompts in the app you are using to initiate the sharing process. [Source: Page 32 | AirDrop, iCloud, and other ways to share]

Q: How do I make a phone call?
A: Making a call on iPhone is as simple as choosing a number in your contacts, tapping one of your favorites or recent calls, or just asking Siri to “call [contact name].” [Source: Page 44 | Phone calls]

Q: How do I take a photo?

Q: My iPhone is frozen, what do I do?
A: If your iPhone is frozen, you can try to force restart it. To do this, hold down the Sleep/Wake button and the Home button at the same time for at least ten seconds, until the Apple logo appears. If that doesn't work, you may need to reset your iPhone or try other troubleshooting steps like restarting the device or forcing an app to quit. [Source: Page 154 | Restart or reset iPhone]

Q: How do I send a text message?
A: To send a text message, start a text conversation by tapping the appropriate icon, then enter a phone number or email address, or tap and choose a contact. After that, tap to enter your text. If you want to include a photo or video, tap the attach media button. [Source: Page 67 | Send and receive messages]

Q: How do I connect to Wi-Fi?
A: To connect to Wi-Fi, go to Settings > Wi-Fi. Then, choose a network by tapping one of the listed networks and entering the password if asked. You can also turn on “Ask to Join Networks” to be prompted when a Wi-Fi network is available. If you want to forget a network you've joined before, tap next to that network and select “Forget this Network.” If you need to join a closed Wi-Fi network, tap Other and enter the name of the closed network along with the required security type and password. Additionally, you can adjust the settings for a Wi-Fi network by tapping next to it to set an HTTP proxy, define static network settings, turn on BootP, or renew the settings provided by a DHCP server. [Source: Page 15 | Connect to Wi-Fi]

Q: How do I back up my iPhone?
A: You can back up your iPhone using iCloud or iTunes. If you choose to back up using iCloud, you cannot also use iTunes for automatic backups, but you can use iTunes for manual backups. iCloud backs up your iPhone daily over Wi-Fi when it is connected to a power source and is locked. 

To turn on iCloud backups, go to Settings > iCloud, log in with your Apple ID and password if required, then go to Storage & Backup and turn on iCloud Backup. 

To turn on backups in iTunes, go to File > Devices > Back Up. 

For more details, refer to the guide on backing up iPhone [Source: Page 156 | Back up iPhone].

Q: How do I use Siri?
A: To use Siri, press and hold the Home button until Siri beeps. You can then ask Siri anything, such as setting a timer or checking movie showtimes. Siri understands natural speech, so you don't need to learn special commands. You can also manually control when Siri listens by holding down the Home button while you speak and releasing it when you're done. Make sure your iPhone is connected to the Internet to use Siri [Source: Page 41 | Make requests].

Q: How do I set a passcode?
A: To set a passcode, go to Settings > Touch ID & Passcode (iPhone 5s) or Settings > Passcode (other models), and set a 4-digit passcode. [Source: Page 37 | Security]

Q: How do I turn on Bluetooth?
A: To turn on Bluetooth, go to Settings > Bluetooth. [Source: Page 35 | Bluetooth devices]

Q: How do I share a photo?
A: To share a photo, view the photo and then tap the share icon. If you don’t see the share icon, tap the screen to show the controls. You can also copy the photo and paste it into an email or text message (MMS or iMessage) [Source: Page 76 | Share photos and videos].

Q: How do I silence my iPhone?
A: You can silence your iPhone by using the Do Not Disturb feature. To turn on Do Not Disturb, swipe up from the bottom edge of the screen to open Control Center, then tap the Do Not Disturb icon. When Do Not Disturb is on, a crescent moon icon appears in the status bar. Note that alarms will still sound even when Do Not Disturb is on. To ensure your iPhone stays silent, you may want to turn off the alarms as well. You can also configure Do Not Disturb settings by going to Settings > Do Not Disturb, where you can schedule quiet hours and allow calls from specific contacts or groups. [Source: Page 32 | Do Not Disturb]

Q: What is Do Not Disturb?
A: Do Not Disturb is a feature that silences calls and notifications automatically on a schedule or manually. It helps block interruptions while sleeping or in meetings by keeping calls and alerts from making any sounds or lighting up the screen. You can turn on Do Not Disturb by swiping up from the bottom edge of the screen to open Control Center and tapping the Do Not Disturb icon. When it is on, a crescent moon icon appears in the status bar. Alarms still sound even when Do Not Disturb is on, so to ensure the iPhone stays silent, it should be turned off. You can configure Do Not Disturb in Settings > Do Not Disturb, where you can schedule quiet hours, allow calls from Favorites or groups, and set whether it silences the iPhone only when locked or even when unlocked. [Source: Page 32 | Do Not Disturb]

Q: Can I schedule it?
A: Yes, you can schedule Do Not Disturb. To do this, go to Settings > Do Not Disturb, where you can set quiet hours and configure other options related to Do Not Disturb. [Source: Page 32 | Do Not Disturb]

Q: Will alarms still go off when it's on?
A: Yes, alarms will still sound even when Do Not Disturb is on. To make sure iPhone stays silent, you would need to turn off Do Not Disturb. [Source: Page 32 | Do Not Disturb]
